The SHAPE agrees and the level does not: every sensor is off by about -2.80 K
together, while their differences from each other are reproduced. That is the
signature of a series resistance nobody modelled -- a contact, a grease line,
a heat sink whose real performance was never disclosed -- not of the
conduction inside the package. It is evidence FOR the solver and AGAINST the
boundary condition as given. Calibrating the boundary on this case and then
quoting the same case as validation would be circular; say which you did.
