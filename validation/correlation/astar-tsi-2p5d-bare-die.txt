THIS CASE CANNOT VALIDATE THE MODEL.
It carries one measurement. A single number fixes a level, and a level is
what an unmodelled contact or an assumed boundary condition already sets,
so any model can be made to agree with it by choosing one input. Such a
case can CALIBRATE; it cannot confirm.
A case earns its verdict by varying in space: sensors far apart, or a hotspot
and a cold corner, at a known power. That is what conduction predicts and what
an unmodelled interface resistance does not.
